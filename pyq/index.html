1. The Evolution of the GATE DA Paper

The Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DA) paper was officially introduced in GATE 2024 by IISc Bangalore. Designed to cater to undergraduate engineers and computing professionals entering modern AI/ML specializations, the paper establishes a dedicated evaluation standard separate from Computer Science and Information Technology (CS/IT).

Because the paper was introduced recently, official previous year papers provide invaluable insight into the depth, rigor, and mathematical maturity expected by the organizing IITs.

2. Exam Structure & Mark Distribution

The GATE DA paper consists of 65 questions carrying a total of 100 marks, to be completed in a 3-hour computer-based test (CBT):

  • General Aptitude (GA): 10 questions (5 one-mark questions + 5 two-mark questions) = 15 marks.
  • Technical Section (DA Core): 55 questions (25 one-mark questions + 30 two-mark questions) = 85 marks.

4. Question Types & Marking Rules

The GATE examination features three distinct question formats:

  • Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ): 4 options with exactly 1 correct option. Negative marking applies: ⅓ penalty for 1-mark questions (−0.33) and ⅔ penalty for 2-mark questions (−0.67).
  • Multiple Select Questions (MSQ): 4 options with one or more correct options. No negative marking and no partial credit; you must select all correct options and no incorrect options to earn marks.
  • Numerical Answer Type (NAT): Enter numerical values using a virtual on-screen keypad within an accepted tolerance window. No negative marking.
