Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. How GATE ML handles cookies, local data, analytics, and advertising.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

GATE ML (hosted at hellogaurav.me) is an independent educational platform created by Gaurav Tiwari for students and engineers preparing for the GATE Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (DA) examination. We respect your privacy: no account registration is required, and study progress is kept directly on your own device.

2. Local Storage and Client-Side Data

Your interactive practice sessions, mock test scores, question attempts, flashcard statuses, and bookmarked topics are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server. You can wipe this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or cache.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

GATE ML contains an AdSense publisher identifier, but advertising is currently disabled while the required Google-certified consent setup is completed. No AdSense script or advertising cookie is intentionally loaded while ad serving is disabled.

  • If advertising is enabled later, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads, subject to users' consent choices.
  • For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, advertising will remain disabled until a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework is configured in the AdSense account.
  • Users can manage local preferences through the Cookie Preferences control, Google Ads Settings, and applicable industry opt-out tools.

For more details on how Google manages data in its advertising products, please consult Google's Advertising Policies and Principles.

4. Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

Google Analytics is currently disabled. If enabled later, it will load only after an analytics preference is granted and will continue to respect your browser's Do Not Track setting.

5. Log Files and Security

Like standard web hosts, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard HTTP access requests (including browser user-agent, request timestamp, and referring URL) solely for operational reliability, DDoS mitigation, and security diagnostics.

6. External Links

Our study materials contain links to external educational resources (such as official IIT GATE portals, academic papers, and Python documentation). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

7. Contact and Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data practices, please email hello@hellogaurav.me.
