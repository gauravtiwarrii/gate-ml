About Gaurav Tiwari

I am Gaurav Tiwari, a computer science and data science practitioner. I built GATE ML on hellogaurav.me to solve a persistent problem in computer science education: preparation for competitive technical exams like GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (DA) was fragmented across unverified question dumps, paywalled test series, and quizzes that ignored real exam-pattern negative marking.

Our Curriculum & Pedagogical Standards

GATE ML is an independent, open-access study platform covering the full theoretical and numerical breadth of the GATE DA syllabus. Our materials are structured across rigorous foundations:

  • Ten-Unit Machine Learning Guide: Over 9,000 lines of comprehensive theory, complete with LaTeX mathematical derivations, scikit-learn implementation comparisons, loss function proofs, and geometric visualizations.
  • 150 Exam-Pattern Practice Questions: Covering Multiple Choice (MCQ with official ⅓ negative penalty), Multi-Select (MSQ with zero partial credit), and Numerical Answer Type (NAT with tolerance bounds).
  • Standard Academic References: Every topic and solution is verified against seminal textbooks including Christopher Bishop's Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning, Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman's The Elements of Statistical Learning, Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest & Stein's Introduction to Algorithms, and Sheldon Ross's A First Course in Probability.
  • Official Scoring Simulation: Timed mock exams simulate the real computer-based test interface with sectional timers, virtual calculators, and mark-for-review palettes.

Editorial Independence & Privacy

Non-Affiliation: GATE ML is entirely independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the organizing IITs, IISc Bangalore, or the National Coordination Board (NCB)-GATE.

Student-First Privacy: We do not require accounts, phone numbers, or passwords. Your study logs, attempts, and bookmarks remain private on your personal device in localStorage.
