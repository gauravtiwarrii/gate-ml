About GATE ML

GATE ML is an independent, free study system for the Machine Learning portion of GATE Data Science & AI: a ten-unit study guide, 150 exam-pattern questions with worked solutions, flashcards, and a mock engine that marks like the real paper. No accounts, no tracking-for-ads, progress stays in your browser.
