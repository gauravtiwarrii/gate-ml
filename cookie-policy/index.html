2. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device by websites you visit. They are commonly used to remember preferences, analyze website traffic, and deliver relevant advertisements.

Local Storage (HTML5) is client-side data storage that allows website data to persist securely within your browser without being transmitted to servers on every HTTP request. GATE ML uses localStorage so your mock test timers, flashcard mastery states, question attempts, and dark/light theme choices remain saved on your device even when offline.

5. How to Control and Opt Out

You have full control over cookie usage:

  • On this website: Click the button at any time to modify your consent.
  • Google Ad Settings: You can manage or disable personalized ads from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry Opt-Out Portals: Opt out of interest-based advertising across multiple ad networks via AboutAds Choices (US), Your Online Choices (EU), or the Network Advertising Initiative.
  • Browser Controls: Most modern browsers allow you to block third-party cookies or wipe stored cookies and site data through settings/privacy menus.
