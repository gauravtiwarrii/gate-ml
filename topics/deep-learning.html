Deep Learning Fundamentals for GATE

Neural networks let the model discover the feature shape itself. GATE tests conceptual clarity — what each architecture assumes about data — more than training tricks.

Important concepts

  • Backpropagation as chain rule + gradient descent; vanishing gradients.
  • Optimisers: SGD with momentum, Adam; regularisation via dropout/weight decay.
  • CNNs: local connectivity, sharing, pooling, translation structure.
  • RNNs/LSTMs vs transformers and self-attention.
  • Transfer learning and when pretraining wins.

FAQ

Why did transformers displace RNNs?

Self-attention gives constant path length plus full parallelism on GPUs — RNNs process tokens serially and lose long-range signal.

Train loss falls but validation rises — what now?

Classic overfitting: early stopping, dropout/weight decay, augmentation, smaller model, and check for distribution mismatch.
