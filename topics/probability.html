Probability for GATE Machine Learning

Probability is the grammar of ML: classifier scores, Naive Bayes tables and confidence statements are conditional probabilities. GATE tests whether you condition correctly and update beliefs with evidence.

Important concepts

  • Conditional probability P(A|B) and Bayes theorem.
  • Independence vs conditional independence (Naive Bayes).
  • Expectation, variance, linearity of expectation.
  • Bernoulli, binomial, Gaussian, exponential distributions.
  • Law of total probability as the Bayes denominator.

GATE relevance

Direct Bayes arithmetic, Naive Bayes tables, P(A|B) vs P(B|A) traps, and likelihood vs posterior framing inside ML questions.

Important formulas

P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B) · P(C|E)=P(E|C)P(C)/P(E) · E[ΣXi]=ΣE[Xi]

Worked example

Test 90% sensitive, 95% specific, 1% sick: P(sick|pos)=0.9·0.01/(0.9·0.01+0.05·0.99)≈15%. Base rates dominate — the exact reasoning Naive Bayes mechanises.

FAQ

Which distributions matter most?

Bernoulli, binomial, Gaussian, exponential — parameters, mean, variance, and where each appears.

Common mistake?

Confusing P(A|B) with P(B|A) and dropping the base rate.
