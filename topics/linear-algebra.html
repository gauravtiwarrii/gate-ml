Linear Algebra for GATE Machine Learning

Linear algebra is the geometry underneath ML: data as vectors, models as matrix multiplications, similarity as dot products, compression as projections. GATE tests the geometric meaning, not rote computation.

Important concepts

  • Vectors, norms, dot products, cosine similarity.
  • Matrix rank, inverse, singularity; solving Xw=y.
  • Eigenvalues/eigenvectors; symmetric matrices and SVD.
  • Projections, least squares as orthogonal projection.
  • PCA: variance-maximising directions, explained variance.
  • Positive definiteness and convexity of quadratics.

GATE relevance

Rank/nullity reasoning, when XᵀX is invertible, eigendecomposition reading, PCA variance questions, and least-squares geometry.

FAQ

Why do eigenvalues appear in ML?

They give the stretching directions of a matrix — variance axes in PCA, stability in optimisation, and rank information.

What does PCA actually keep?

The orthogonal directions of maximum variance; explained-variance ratios say how much information each keeps.
