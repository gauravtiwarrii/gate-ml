Frequently asked questions

Common questions about GATE ML — what it is, who it's for, and how it works.

What is GATE ML?

A free, working study system for GATE Data Science & AI (ML portion): 10-unit guide, 150 exam-pattern questions, 60 flashcards, timed mocks with real marking. Try it at Practice.

Who is it for?

CS & allied students and engineers preparing for GATE DA. Assumes basic Python + college maths.

Is the entire platform free to use?

Yes. All 10 Machine Learning guide units, the complete 150-question practice bank, flashcards, and the timed mock exam engine are 100% free and open-access. No payment, credit card, or account registration is ever required.

How does the scoring and negative marking work?

Our mock tests and practice drills apply official GATE rules: 1-mark MCQs deduct ⅓ marks for wrong answers; 2-mark MCQs deduct ⅔ marks. MSQs and NAT questions carry zero negative penalty, though MSQs require all correct options to be selected with no partial credit.

Are the questions aligned with the official syllabus?

Yes. Every question is mapped directly to the official GATE Data Science & AI (DA) syllabus released by the organizing IITs. Review the complete subject breakdown in our Syllabus Index.
