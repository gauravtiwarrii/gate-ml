3. The Official CBT Question Palette

The simulation uses the exact color codes mandated by the GATE organizing committee:

  • Grey: You have not visited the question yet.
  • Red: You have visited the question but have not answered it.
  • Green: You have answered the question. (Counts towards evaluation).
  • Purple: You have not answered the question, but have marked it for review.
  • Purple with Green Indicator: The question has been answered AND marked for review. (Important GATE Rule: This response WILL be evaluated during final scoring).

4. Recommended 3-Pass Exam Strategy

Top percentile scorers recommend structuring your 180-minute examination into three disciplined cycles:

  1. Cycle 1 — Fast Wins (Minutes 0 to 45): Sweep through the entire question paper. Solve all high-confidence theoretical MCQs and straightforward 1-mark aptitude problems immediately. Mark computational questions for Cycle 2. Never spend more than 2 minutes on any single question during this pass.
  2. Cycle 2 — Numerical Core (Minutes 45 to 135): Tackle the 2-mark technical problems, numerical integrations, Bayes updates, and algorithm traces. Work with the virtual on-screen calculator carefully, double-checking intermediate arithmetic.
  3. Cycle 3 — Edge Cases & Verification (Minutes 135 to 180): Revisit all questions marked for review. Verify that you have not left MSQs partially completed and ensure no accidental clicks exist on uncertain MCQs where negative marking penalties apply.
