Calculus & Optimization (DA §3)

Official (2027 PDF): Functions of a single variable, limit, continuity and differentiability, Taylor series, maxima and minima, optimization involving a single variable.

1. Objectives

Evaluate limits; test continuity/differentiability; use Taylor expansion; find and classify stationary points.

2. Prerequisites

Algebra, functions, basic differentiation.

3. Concept

Differentiability implies continuity; converse is false (|x| at 0). Second-derivative test: f'=0 and f''>0 → local minimum; f''<0 → maximum; f''=0 → inspect further.

4. Formulas

Taylor: f(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x−a)+f''(a)(x−a)²/2!+…
Stationary: f'(c)=0; classify via f''(c).

5. Worked examples

E1. f(x)=x³−3x²+2: f'=3x²−6x=0 → x=0,2. f''=6x−6: f''(0)=−6 max, f''(2)=6 min.

E2. limx→0 sin x / x = 1 (standard; use series sin x=x−x³/6+…).

6. GATE applications

Single-variable optimization appears directly; Taylor remainder bounds error of approximations.
